2.1 Obesity Profile — OpenAlex Mismatch (OPEN)
------------------------------------------------
Seed paper "Recognizing Obesity and Comorbidities in Sparse Data" was
matched by OpenAlex to a completely wrong paper:
  Title stored: "0957 + 561 A, B: twin quasistellar objects or gravitational lens?"
  OpenAlex ID: W2036247675
  Body text: correct (obesity NLP content)
  Abstract: nearly empty (13 chars)

This causes the reranker to promote astrophysics papers to the top.

Fix: Delete this seed paper and re-upload the PDF, or manually correct
the title/abstract in the database. Then re-run gather for the profile.

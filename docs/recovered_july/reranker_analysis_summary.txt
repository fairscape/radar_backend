========================================================================
Overall Conclusions
========================================================================

1. Article mode works well when seed papers are thematically coherent
   and correctly matched (type_1_diabetes: clear improvement).

2. Article mode captures cross-disciplinary signals that topic mode
   cannot (infectious_disease: promotes fisheries + disease intersection
   papers that topic-name queries would miss).

3. Article mode is vulnerable to data quality issues:
   - OpenAlex mismatch (obesity: astrophysics title on obesity paper)
     causes catastrophic failure.
   - Seed papers with very long body_text can dominate query direction
     when seed paper themes are not fully aligned.

4. The selector's inherent ML bias (type_2_diabetes) is partially but
   not fully corrected by the reranker.

5. MedCPT cross-encoder has limited precision on non-medical content,
   producing noise in cross-domain profiles.

Recommendations:
- Fix the obesity seed paper OpenAlex mismatch before re-evaluating.
- Consider input validation during upload to flag title/body_text
  semantic mismatch (e.g., cosine similarity check between title
  embedding and body_text embedding).
- For cross-domain profiles, the alpha/beta weights may need
  per-profile tuning, or a hybrid topic+article query mode.
